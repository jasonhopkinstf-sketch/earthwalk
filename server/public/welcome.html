Welcome to EarthWalk

The whole real Earth, drawn as a cartoon you can walk, ride and fly through, together.

1What EarthWalk is

EarthWalk is a copy of the real world. The streets, buildings, hills, rivers, railways and parks are where they really are, taken from real maps, and painted in a friendly cartoon style.

You are an animal: a fox, a bear, a rabbit, a cat, an owl or a frog. You can walk down your own street, fly over Paris, or take a taxi to the pyramids.

There is nothing to win and nothing can hurt you. It is a place to explore, alone or with family and friends.

Famous places and big cities look most like themselves. An ordinary house is in the right spot with the right outline, but its colour and roof are made up.

2Your first five minutes

  1. Pick a name and an animal, then choose Enter the world. People in the same room see each other, so use the room name your family uses.
  2. Walk with W A S D. Hold the left mouse button and drag to look around. Space jumps.
  3. Press Tab. That opens a list of everything you can do, to click. You never need to type a command.
  4. Press G for "Show me". Pick anything on the list and you are taken straight there.
  5. Press F to fly, and F again to land. Space goes up and C goes down.

The row of buttons along the bottom of the screen holds the things used most. The little map in the corner shows friends, doors you can go in, parked cars and train stops.

3Go anywhere on Earth

Go to a place

Type a town, a street or a famous place, such as "Sydney Opera House", and you fly there and watch the journey.

Show me

A ready list of good places to see and things to try. Press G.

Ask the guide

Press Q and ask in plain words: "where am I?", "is there a shop near here?", "how do I get to the beach?". The answer comes with buttons that take you there.

Fly

Press F and look at the world from above. The higher you go, the further you see.

4Ride and drive

Walk up to almost anything that moves and press E to hop on. Press E again to hop off.

Animals: horses, cows, sheep, camels, elephants, giraffes, kangaroos and many more.

Wheels and water: cars, vans, fire engines, tractors, rowing boats and sailing boats.

Trains: wait where a train stops, hop on, and watch the country go by.

Your own car comes when you press C. A taxi comes when you press X and drives you where you ask.

While you ride, V changes the view: your seat, from above, or from high over the rooftops.

5Talk to anything

Stand beside an animal, a car, a train, a tree or a house and press T. They all answer.

Tell it what to do

"Follow me", "stay", or "take me to the beach". A horse or a car will carry you there by itself.

Feed a hungry animal

A thought bubble over an animal shows the food it would like. Feed it from your lunch box and you earn coins.

Chat with people

Press Enter and type. Everybody in your room reads it.

6Go inside buildings

A yellow COME IN sign over a door means you can walk in. Press E at the door.

Most houses open. So do shops, cafés, offices, shopping malls, churches, mosques, temples, schools, hospitals, factories, warehouses and petrol stations.

There are people inside doing what they do there: teaching, cooking, working a machine. Press T to talk to them. Beside a real shop or café, your lunch box shows what it sells.

7A house of your own

Make it yours

Walk into a house nobody lives in and press H. Your name goes over the door, and H takes you home from anywhere.

Furnish it

Inside your house, H opens the panel: walls, floors, sofas, beds and rugs, bought with coins. Prizes you win go on show.

Keep a garden

Up to four young animals live by your front door. Feed them once a day and when they are grown they give eggs, milk or wool.

Use a real floor plan

Signed in, you can upload a picture of a real house's floor plan and have the inside of your house built from it, stairs and all.

8Games, and your passport

Press P for your passport. It keeps everything you have done.

Today's tasks

Three small things to do each day, such as "ride a kilometre on anything".

Treasure hunts and expeditions

Follow clues to a place, or set off on a longer journey.

Stamps, riddles and hats

A stamp for each country you visit, riddles to solve, and hats to buy with your coins.

Levels

Your progress bar fills as you explore, eat well and finish tasks. A full bar is a new level.

Races

Press R to set up a race along the roads where you stand. Friends press R to join.

Tag, hide and seek, a round-up

Start one from the menu where you stand. In a round-up you lead animals into a pen.

A companion and its tricks

Feed enough hungry animals and one becomes your companion. Press K to teach it tricks or set up an agility course.

Your lunch box

Press B. Five hearts, one each for fruit, vegetables, grains, protein and dairy. Eating a bit of everything keeps them full.

9Play together

Rooms

People in the same room see each other, wherever on Earth they are standing.

Invite somebody

"Invite somebody" in the menu copies a link. Send it, and it brings them into your room.

Go to a friend

"Go to a friend" lands you beside them. Then ride in the same car, race, or visit each other's houses.

Sign in to keep things

Signing in with Google keeps your passport, coins, house and home between visits and between computers.

10Real sun, moon and weather

The sky over each place is the real one: if it is night and raining in London right now, it is night and raining in EarthWalk's London, with lamps lit in the windows. If you would rather have a fine day everywhere, the menu has "always daytime" and "a fine day".

12Good to know

Two ways to play

In a web browser, right here, or with the EarthWalk game for Windows from the download page. Both show the same world and the same people.

You need a keyboard and mouse

Phones and tablets are not supported yet.

A new place takes a moment

The first time anybody visits somewhere, the world there has to be built from the map. It is quicker the next time.

Got an idea?

"Suggest something" in the menu sends your idea to the people who make EarthWalk.
